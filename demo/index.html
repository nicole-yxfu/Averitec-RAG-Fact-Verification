A fact-checker that knows when to ask a human

For each claim, the system searches the web, asks an LLM for a verdict, then decides whether to trust that verdict or send the claim to a person for review. Pick a claim, follow the four steps, then reveal what professional fact-checkers concluded.

Read the numbers on this page with one correction. The search results shown here are the original ones, and for about half the claims they include the published fact-check of that claim, which leaks the answer. With fact-check sites removed, the same method is 43% accurate answering every claim and 74% accurate on the half it is most confident about. Details on GitHub →
